# Into The Facade roadmap

## Current midterm scope

Urban exploration supported by open-source intelligence: three researched industrial properties, original evidence and direct source links, ownership and permission channels, documented hazards, and dated signs of current use. A closed business is a lead, not an access grant or a vacancy verdict.

- Three illustrated decks, sequential left/right card navigation, and an active deck centered in the left index.
- Overview, Evidence and read-only explorer Field notes; export includes the research sources.
- Geographic context, real county parcels and the source-tagged service-road discrepancy.
- Separate recorded-activity and security-evidence indicators. No invented guards, cameras, dogs, patrol patterns or claims of unrestricted access.
- Optional background soundtrack, requested only after visitor interaction, with pause/volume controls and automatic pause in a hidden tab.

## Future update

### Saved places and collections — disabled

Saved navigation, bookmark buttons and save/filter handlers are removed from the current website. Existing `itf:saved` browser data is left untouched for a possible migration. Published explorer accounts remain available as a separate feature.

Before re-enabling Saved:

- Define collections around researched sites and permission status.
- Provide clear add/remove controls and keyboard feedback.
- Support export/import and explain the limits of browser-only persistence.
- Revalidate saved records when source dates or property use change.
- Decide whether account sync is justified; do not promise it without storage and authentication.

### Personal field-note editor and submissions — future update

The current Field Notes card displays prior explorer accounts. Stockyard includes a submission attributed to **Anonymous explorer**, with recollection and unverified allegations clearly separated from documentary evidence. The other sites have no submitted explorer accounts yet; no accounts are fabricated.

The personal textarea, Save note action and browser-note reads are disabled. Existing `itf:note:*` data remains untouched. Future work includes dated observations, attribution/privacy options, source attachments, correction and review handling, and an explicit distinction between submitted claims and verified findings.

### Research coverage

- Confirm authorized visitor arrangements and any designated public observation points with the property controller.
- Date every submitted observation and attach its source image; separate records, observations and inference.
- Expand environmental, structural and legal records. Animal or security claims require site-specific evidence; lack of a report is not an absence finding.
- Add owner/occupant corrections and periodic source rechecks before treating the atlas as current field guidance.

### Soundtrack delivery

- Review dedicated media hosting if traffic grows; the initial static demo serves a compressed copy of the supplied recording.
- Add additional tracks and an explicit audio settings panel only if needed.
